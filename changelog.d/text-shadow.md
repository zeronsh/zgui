- **Text shadows** (zgx, zgx-script): `Styled::text_shadow(x, y, blur, color)` and `text_shadows`, Luau `text_shadow = { x, y, blur, color }` or a list, as CSS's `text-shadow`: the glyphs again under them, moved and tinted, inherited by the text inside. `Scene::set_text_shadows` for scenes built directly. A blurred shadow draws sharp until its blur lands.
