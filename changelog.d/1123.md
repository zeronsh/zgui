- **glTF lights and cameras; spot lights, and lights that follow objects, in 3D scenes**: a model's `KHR_lights_punctual` lights (suns, bulbs and spots, glTF's units as zgx-3d's) are added with it, each following its node, and `Placed::lights()` gives them; `Placed::cameras(&world)` gives its cameras where its nodes are now, for `World::set_camera`. `World::set_light_parent(light, Some(id))` places any light in an object's frame, and `World::set_spot(light, Some(Spot::new(direction, inner, outer)))` shines a point light as a cone. A frame now shades with the eight point lights nearest the camera's target, where it took four. A world using none of these binds nothing new.
