- **A covered window stops drawing on Windows** (zgx-desktop): as on macOS and X11, a window nothing of which can be seen, or on another virtual desktop, does no drawing, and `ui.window.shown` is false there; on the way its menus and popovers close, as they do when it is minimized. A window that may show through (translucent, click-through, acrylic) never counts as covering it. Automated tests that need a covered window to go on drawing set `ZGX_RENDER_WHEN_COVERED=1`.
