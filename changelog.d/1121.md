- **`zgx inspect` and `zgx test` act only on what shows** (zgx-script): a closed popover's, menu's or modal's content stays in the scene, unseen, and `click`, `hover`, `right-click`, `drag`, `scroll`, `snapshot` and `wait` took it as if it showed, landing on whatever was under it. They now fail with `doesn't show … (open what holds it first)`; `find` and `tree` still list it, marked `hidden`. A test that clicked something closed now fails where it used to pass.
