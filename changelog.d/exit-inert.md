- **What leaves takes no clicks** (zgx-script): a `ui.each` row removed from its list, and what a `ui.presence` lets go of, play their `exit` where they were but the pointer passes through them to what's under, so a fast second click acts on what's still there, never on data already gone. A focused row leaving gives focus to the next row (else the one before, else the nearest focusable around it), Enter and Space never activate it, and assistive technology no longer offers it.
