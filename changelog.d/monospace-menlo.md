- **`monospace` is Menlo on macOS** (zgx-gpu): every app's monospaced text now has a face of its own on macOS, as on Windows (Cascadia Mono or Consolas) and Linux. cosmic-text named Noto Sans Mono, which macOS lacks, so each word took the fixed-width face with most of its characters; Latin text already came out in Menlo. A word mixing Latin with characters Menlo lacks (Chinese, Japanese, Hebrew) now takes its Latin letters from Menlo and the rest from another face, where it could take all of them from one.
